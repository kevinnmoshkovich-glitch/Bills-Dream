The documented franchise disasters, with receipts. Tap any link — it's all real.

JAN 1991 · SUPER BOWL XXV · GIANTS 20, BILLS 19

Wide Right

Heavily favored Buffalo ran into the Parcells–Belichick Giants, who smothered the no-huddle and watched Norwood's 47-yarder sail wide right. The Giants took the trophy; Buffalo took a two-word curse.

1991–1994

0–4 In Super Bowls

The only franchise ever to lose four straight Super Bowls — including a 52–17 demolition with a record nine turnovers. Haven't been back since.

JAN 2000

Music City Miracle

Lead with 16 seconds left, then a 75-yard trick lateral return ends the season. Fans still call it a forward pass. The refs and an NFL Films analysis disagree.

2000–2016

The 17-Year Drought

Seventeen straight seasons without a playoff berth — the longest active drought in major American pro sports. An entire generation grew up without one January game.

JAN 2022

13 Seconds

Up three with thirteen seconds left; lost in overtime without touching the ball. Most teams need four quarters to break your heart. Buffalo is efficient.

JAN 2025

Kansas City, Again

32–29 at Arrowhead — the fourth playoff elimination by KC in five years. Dominant against the Chiefs every fall, winless against them every winter.

JAN 2026 · THE FRESHEST ONE

Denver In OT — Then They Fired The Coach

Fourth divisional-round exit in five years; the owner saw Josh Allen's face and fired McDermott — nine seasons, 98 wins, zero Super Bowls. The rebuild begins where every Bills season ends: in January, in pieces.
