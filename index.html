Week 1 brief

The Giants opened with a 28–20 win over Dallas on Sunday night. Jaxson Dart was 23/29 for 230 yards — a 134.2 passer rating — and the run game did the heavy lifting: 165 yards at 4.5 a carry, led by Cam Skattebo (18 carries, 81 yards). Isaiah Likely led the receivers with 8 catches for 78. The number that decided it: 81.82% on third down. Tremaine Edmunds led the defense with 8 tackles.

The Bills won 36–31 at Houston in a game that stayed loud all afternoon. Josh Allen threw for 334 yards on 20/29 and Dalton Kincaid was the problem Houston never solved — 5 catches, 130 yards, 26.0 a catch. Buffalo did it without the ground game (86 rushing yards) and while converting only 33.33% on third down — they simply scored when they had the ball. Terrel Bernard paced the defense with 12 tackles.

The split so far: Buffalo is the more explosive team — eight more points and 104 more passing yards a game. New York is the more efficient one — nearly double Buffalo's third-down rate and 79 more yards on the ground. Two ways of winning the same number of games.
