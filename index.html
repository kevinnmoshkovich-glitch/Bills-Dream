JAN 1991 · SUPER BOWL XXV · GIANTS 20, BILLS 19

Wide Right

Buffalo was heavily favored. Then the New York Giants happened. The Parcells–Belichick defense strangled the famous no-huddle offense and kept it off the field for over 40 minutes, and Scott Norwood's 47-yarder to win it sailed wide right. The Giants left with the trophy. Buffalo left with a two-word tombstone inscription.

1991–1994 · SUPER BOWLS XXV–XXVIII

Four Funerals, No Wedding

The only franchise in NFL history to play — and lose — four consecutive Super Bowls. 0–4. They haven't been back since. The Cowboys beat them twice, once 52–17 in a game featuring a Super Bowl record nine Buffalo turnovers. "The Buffalo Bills of ______" became the universal insult for chokers in every sport. That's not a legacy. That's a warning label.

JAN 2000 · AFC WILD CARD

The Music City Miracle

Lead taken with 16 seconds left. Then Tennessee ran a trick lateral kickoff back 75 yards to win it. Bills fans have spent 26 years insisting it was a forward pass. The refs said no. An NFL Films computer analysis said no. Reality keeps saying no. Grief has five stages; Buffalo lives permanently in denial.

2000–2016 · THE DARK AGES

Seventeen Years In The Ground

Seventeen consecutive seasons without a playoff berth — the longest active drought in all of major American pro sports. An entire generation was born, learned to walk, learned to talk, got driver's licenses, and graduated high school without ever seeing the Bills play in January. Child services was never called. It should have been.

JAN 2022 · AFC DIVISIONAL

13 Seconds

Up 36–33 with thirteen seconds on the clock — and they still found a way. Mahomes needed two plays to reach field goal range, and Buffalo lost in overtime without ever touching the ball. Most teams need four quarters to break your heart. The Bills are efficient: they can do it in the time it takes to microwave nothing.

JAN 2025 · AFC CHAMPIONSHIP

Kansas City. Again. Always.

A 32–29 loss at Arrowhead — the fourth time since January 2021 that the Chiefs personally ended Buffalo's season. The Bills beat the Chiefs in the regular season constantly and have never once beaten them in the playoffs. Regular season Bills will take your money all fall. Playoff Bills are a different business entirely — and the product is grief.

THE RÉSUMÉ

Big Blue Doesn't Tease

While Buffalo perfected the art of taking your Sundays and giving nothing back, the New York Giants were collecting hardware: Super Bowls XXI, XXV, XLII, and XLVI. The second one was won directly over Buffalo's corpse. The Giants don't promise. The Giants deliver.

FEB 2008 · SUPER BOWL XLII

They Killed Perfection

The Giants walked into the desert against an 18-0 Patriots team one win from immortality — and murdered the perfect season with the Helmet Catch. Then they beat Brady and Belichick in the Super Bowl again four years later, just to prove it wasn't luck. The Bills spent those same two decades getting swept by New England and calling it a rivalry.

EVERY JANUARY

Closers vs. Ghosts

Giants in Super Bowls: 4–1. Bills in Super Bowls: 0–4. One franchise shows up in the dark and finishes the job. The other is the dark. When history needed a giant-killer, it called New York. When history needed a punchline, Buffalo picked up on the first ring — the only ring they'll ever get.

Field Report: Bills Mafia
The only fanbase in professional sports whose signature celebration is a felony against furniture. While Giants fans watch championships, Bills fans hurl their own bodies through folding tables in a frozen parking lot — before kickoff, in 12°F, sober-ish. The tables committed no crime. The tables did not go wide right. But every Sunday in Orchard Park, another innocent Costco special is sacrificed to a football god who has never once answered. — Correspondent embedded in an Orchard Park parking lot, presumed concussed
  • Grim math: More Bills fans have gone through tables than Bills teams have gone through to a Super Bowl since 1994.
  • Medical note: Jumping through a table and rooting for the Bills have identical outcomes — a brief high, a hard landing, and something broken by February.
  • Anthropology: Scientists believe the table-smashing is a grief ritual. The Bills provide unlimited grief. The ritual continues.
The Fine Print of Fandom
  • The business model: Sixty-six years of selling hope by the season and never once delivering the product. Every fall they take your heart, your Sundays, and your money — and every January they leave you at the altar. Football's longest, coldest tease.
  • The pattern: Regular season heroics, playoff funerals. Set your watch to it.
  • The cure: The New York Giants remain proudly in the New York metro area with four Lombardi Trophies. No passport, no folding table, no therapy required.
