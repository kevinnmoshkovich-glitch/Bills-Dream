How this is worked out

The chances. Every game still to be played has a win probability published by ESPN’s model. The rest of the season is played out 20,000 times with those numbers, the league’s seeding rules are applied to each finished season, and a club’s chance is the share of those seasons it got in.

Officially in, knocked out. Never taken from the played-out seasons. A club is marked only when the league has marked it, or when plain arithmetic leaves no other result.

Tiebreakers. Applied in the league’s order: head to head, division record, conference record, strength of victory, strength of schedule. The “common games” step is not applied, so a very tight tie can fall differently from the league’s own answer.

What it does not know. Injuries, a hot streak, or a club resting players in Week 18. A win this week does not make a club stronger next week in these numbers. No simulated game ends in a tie.

Seeds shown as “today” are the league’s own, as published. The “if they win” columns carry about a point of noise either way.
