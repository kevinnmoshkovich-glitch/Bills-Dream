Best chances ranked on production, not on the model

What this model does not know. It has no injury data feeding the numbers, no matchup, no defence faced, no weather and no depth chart. The percentages are the sportsbook’s own line spread across thresholds — the book has priced all of that in, but we are not computing any of it. Nor is a player’s own history in the probability: the spread comes from a generic league figure per stat, not from him. His record only affects the ORDER of the suggestions below. Injury statuses are shown where the league lists one, and a player carrying one is never suggested.

Read this before trusting the order. Ranking by the modelled probability alone would be circular - the model puts the median at the book’s line, so the highest probability is always the lowest threshold, for every player, every time. That says nothing about who is likely to beat their number. These rank instead on the one thing the model does not know: whether the player has actually been clearing it. With 8 games played that evidence is thin and the order still leans mostly on the model - it starts meaning something around week six. A shortlist to look at, not a verdict.
