Read this before trusting the order. Ranking by the modelled probability alone would be circular - the model puts the median at the book’s line, so the highest probability is always the lowest threshold, for every player, every time. That says nothing about who is likely to beat their number. These rank instead on the one thing the model does not know: whether the player has actually been clearing it. With 1 game played that evidence is thin and the order still leans mostly on the model - it starts meaning something around week six. A shortlist to look at, not a verdict.
